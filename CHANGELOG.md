# Changelog

New, changed, fixed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## 1.0.1

- New: mod picture in Obsidian.
- Changed: a hidden cape is hidden again twice a second instead of every frame, so most frames make no engine call. New capes are still hidden before the first frame they would show.

## 1.0.0

- New: hides the cape of every Helldiver, including other players and bots.
- New: capes stay hidden when other mods, such as Photo Mode, try to show them.
- New: Hide capes option, also in the pause menu under Mods.
